Tubedash privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

Tubedash is a personal tool that I (iamcstevenson) build and run for my own use. It isn't offered to the public. This policy explains what it does with data, including data from Google and YouTube, for anyone who signs in to it.

Data from Google (YouTube API Services)

Tubedash uses YouTube API Services. Connecting YouTube is optional and only needed to save videos to a playlist. When you connect, Google asks you to grant the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube (“manage your YouTube account”). Tubedash uses it only for the following:

DataWhat Tubedash does with it
Your channel's nameShows which account is connected.
Your playlists' names and IDsFinds the playlist named “TubeDash” (or the name set in its settings). It never creates, renames or deletes playlists.
The items in that one playlistLists them to show which videos are saved, adds a video when you click Save, and removes it when you click it again.
A sign-in token from GoogleLets Tubedash do the above without asking you to sign in each time.

Tubedash does not access your watch history, subscriptions, comments, uploads, other playlists, email address, or any other Google or YouTube data, and it never uploads, edits or deletes videos.

How data is stored

  • The sign-in token, the playlist's ID and name, and which videos are saved are stored in Tubedash's database on the private server it runs on. Only my own devices can reach that server, over a private network.
  • The database is backed up nightly. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server and are kept for 14 days.
  • The rest of the database holds public information: the channels followed, their public videos and descriptions, the summaries Tubedash wrote, and which videos were marked watched.

Sharing

Tubedash does not sell, rent, transfer or share Google user data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it except me, as the operator, when needed for security or to fix a problem.

To write summaries, Tubedash sends the public link, title and description of a video to Google's Gemini API. This never includes your Google account data.

Tubedash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect in Tubedash revokes its access with Google and deletes the sign-in token from its database.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections or the Google security settings page.
  • To have any remaining data deleted, open an issue (below); it's deleted within 7 days, and from backups within the following 14 days.

Google and YouTube

Using Tubedash's YouTube features means you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and tracking

Tubedash and this website use no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
